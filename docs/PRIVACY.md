# FreakFM Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

FreakFM is an offline music player. It is developed by Alferid Hassen.

## What FreakFM accesses
- **Audio files on your device** (permission `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on older Android) — used only to build your music library and play it. Files are never uploaded.
- **Notifications / foreground service** — used to show playback controls and keep music playing in the background.

## What leaves your device
- When you open the lyrics screen, the **song title, artist, album and duration** are sent to the public lyrics services LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and Lyrics.ovh to look up lyrics. No account, advertising ID or device identifier is sent.
- Nothing else is transmitted. FreakFM has **no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no user accounts**.

## What is stored on your device
Your library index, likes, play counts, playlists, folder choices and cached lyrics/artwork are stored locally in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Sharing
Lyric cards you create are shared only when you choose to share them, using Android's share sheet.

## Children
FreakFM does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
