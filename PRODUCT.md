# PRODUCT.md

## Product Truth: FreakFM (Spotify Edition)

### 1. Vision & Purpose
FreakFM is a high-performance, offline-first music player application for Windows Desktop and Android Mobile. It combines the sleek, beloved visual language and user experience of Spotify with instant local storage indexing (< 1 second for 1,000+ songs), free synchronized lyrics streaming, gapless background audio playback, and system media integration.

### 2. Target Audience
Music enthusiasts who desire the premium, tactile dark-mode aesthetic of Spotify for their personal offline audio library without advertisements, subscriptions, or telemetry.

### 3. Core Capabilities
- **Spotify Aesthetic**: Deep pitch-black base (`#121212`), elevated card surfaces (`#181818`, `#242424`), iconic Spotify green accents (`#1DB954`), Spotify pill category filters, 2-column quick play tiles, and modern typography.
- **Ultra-Fast Library Scanner**: Android MediaStore + high-throughput multi-directory background isolate filesystem indexing.
- **Synchronized Lyrics Engine**: Live, free LRC lyrics fetched from LRCLIB & Lyrics.ovh with pixel-accurate auto-scrolling, manual scroll grace period, and instant resume sync.
- **Full Playback Controls**: Shuffle, repeat, repeat one, playback speed, master volume, sleep timer, background audio with notification panel controls and lock screen art.
- **Cross-Platform Responsive UX**: Adapts seamlessly from mobile touch devices (Android) to desktop widescreen (Windows).
